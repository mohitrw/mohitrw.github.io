Work Process

Understanding Requirements

Before starting development, I review the project goals, target audience, and design specifications.

Planning & Structure

I organize the information and plan the overall experience before moving into detailed design. I define the page structure, content hierarchy, user flow, and key sections to create a clear and easy-to-navigate experience.

Responsive Development

I turn the planned experience into a visual interface in Figma, focusing on layout, typography, spacing, components, and responsive behavior. When needed, I bring the design to life using Wix Studio, HTML, CSS, or AI-powered tools.

Performance & Accessibility

I review the design across different screen sizes and focus on clear typography, readable content, consistent spacing, proper visual hierarchy, and accessible interactions. I also optimize images and other visual elements where needed.

Testing & Refinement

I test the final experience across different screen sizes, review the overall usability and visual consistency, and refine anything that feels unclear or out of place. I continue iterating until the design feels polished and ready to present or use.

Client Communication & Handover

I keep communication clear throughout the project, share progress and design decisions, take feedback, and make revisions where needed. Once everything is finalized, I prepare the project for handover and make sure the final outcome matches the agreed requirements.

UX DESIGN

I approach design with the user in mind, using research and competitive references to understand needs, behaviors, and opportunities. I use tools like Pinterest, Mobbin, Dribbble, ChatGPT, and Obsidian to explore and organize ideas before turning insights into clearer user experiences.

UI DESIGN

Figma is my primary design tool for creating clean, responsive interfaces with strong visual hierarchy, typography, spacing, and consistency. I also create personalized logos and visual assets when needed, using a combination of Figma and Canva.
